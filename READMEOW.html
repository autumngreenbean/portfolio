About This Collection

These documents represent snapshots of computing culture from the 1990s and early 2000s. They capture the excitement, wonder, and creativity of an era when digital media was rapidly evolving.

Each item has been carefully curated and digitized for preservation. Many are sourced from Archive.org, a nonprofit library dedicated to preserving digital and physical culture.

Technical Details

PDF Viewer

PDFs are rendered using pdf.js, providing smooth zoom, pan, and multi-page navigation. The viewer caches recently viewed documents for fast switching.
